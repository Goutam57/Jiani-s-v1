The Stanmore Place story

From a Christmas event to a weekly pop-up

Jiani’s was invited and sponsored to cater for a Stanmore Place residents’ Christmas event. The response was overwhelmingly positive. What began as a one-off gathering evolved into a monthly event and eventually into a weekly pop-up driven by resident demand.

Today, Friday evenings have become about more than collecting pizza. Residents meet neighbours they may never have spoken to before. Conversations start, friendships form and a stronger sense of community is created.

It’s no longer just about pizza. It’s about people.

Investing in the next generation

Young people, real experience

Local school-age residents have volunteered to help at the pop-up, giving them first-hand experience of a small business.

Through serving customers and working as part of the team, they gain experience in communication, teamwork, customer service and entrepreneurship while building confidence and self-esteem.

A business with purpose

Food can do more than satisfy hunger

It can create conversations, build relationships and strengthen communities. That is what makes the Stanmore Place pop-up so meaningful, and it is a spirit we want to carry wherever Jiani’s goes.
