Allergen information

All our pizzas are prepared in an environment that contains gluten, dairy, nuts and eggs. Whilst every effort is made to separate vegetarian and vegan pizzas, cross-contamination may occur whilst preparing the order. If you have any concerns or suffer from allergies or food intolerance, please contact us. Menu items are subject to availability. Images give a general indication of the finished product and can vary due to the artisan nature of the product.
