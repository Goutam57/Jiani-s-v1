Short founder story

From Lockdown to Local Favourite

Jiani’s started during lockdown in 2021, making pizzas at home for family. I wanted to move beyond shop-bought dough and learn how to create something genuinely good from scratch.

After months of learning, testing, mistakes and refinement, I created a pizza I was proud of and shared it with a few neighbours. They loved it. A handful of pizzas became small local orders, then a pop-up, then a weekly community fixture and a growing events business.

Our craft

Crafted With Intention

Our mission is to craft exceptional pizzas using the finest Italian ingredients, with a commitment to quality and authenticity, delivering fresh, flavourful and easily digestible pizza that brings joy, unity and memorable moments.

Great pizza starts with the dough. Freshly made from a blend of Italian flours with vital wheat germ and slowly pre-fermented for 48 hours, it develops naturally into a light, airy crust full of flavour, fragrance and texture, without leaving you feeling heavy.

Homemade sauce using D.O.P. San Marzano tomatoes, fresh fragrant basil, Italian extra virgin olive oil and a touch of salt. No additives, purées or artificial ingredients, so it stays fresh, juicy and natural.

Neapolitan style cut fiordilatte mozzarella made from Red Tractor Assured cows’ milk, with locally sourced ingredients chosen for flavour, quality and consistency.

Every pizza is stone-baked at intense heat and cooked in minutes, creating the light, beautifully blistered crust and freshly baked finish that defines a Jiani’s pizza.

The Heart Behind The Heat

Jiani’s is built on passion, consistency and a love for creating something meaningful. Every pizza reflects a journey, from learning and refining to sharing and bringing people together.

Jiani’s was never intended to become just another pizza business. It began as a personal challenge to learn a craft of a food that I enjoy, create something I could be proud of and bring joy to the people around me. What followed was a journey of continuous learning, mistakes, refinement and growth.

Building Jiani’s has also become part of my own journey: developing discipline, consistency, confidence and my entrepreneurial mindset. What started as a passion project has become a business, a community and a platform for creating experiences that make people happy.
