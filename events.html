FAQs

Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Send your date through the enquiry form and we’ll confirm availability.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Our menu is veggie-inspired with vegan options. Allergen details are on the menu page; tell us about any needs when you enquire.

What space and set-up do you need?

Share your venue or garden details and guest numbers and we’ll confirm what’s required.

How long is the service?

This depends on guest numbers and your timings. Tell us in your enquiry and we’ll plan it with you.

What if the weather is bad?

Let us know whether your event is indoors or outdoors and we’ll discuss contingency plans when we confirm.
